Cavity refine: ideas to try (from review of the "bridge cap" suggestion)

Keep
- Cap, do not plug. Our spawn seeds the fruit at the max clearance point deep in the pocket and nudges it
  inward, so it wedges and leaves two narrow wedges. A fruit that spans the mouth and sits on both rims hides
  the pocket and costs one fruit.
- Mouth extraction: mouth voxels = unreachable free voxels that touch tool-reachable free voxels. Cluster them;
  per cluster get center m, width W (smallest PCA extent), slot direction (largest PCA extent) and outward
  normal n (mean container sdf gradient, or mean direction from cavity into reachable air).
- Cap kind filter: smallest extent of the fruit > ~1.25 * W (mirror of the current plug filter, which needs
  inscribed radius <= clearance + 0.4). Try kinds from smallest to largest; stop at the first that clears the
  objective. Fewer and smaller fruit.
- Cap pose: long axis of the fruit along the slot direction, rolls about it; center at m + h*n.

Change from the suggestion
- No analytic h from sphere formulas (fruit are not spheres, rims are not circles). Start the fruit outside the
  mouth along n and bisect h on the exact penetration test (Pen in CavityRefine.cpp) to the first contact.
  Deterministic, no PBD, no gravity. Optional few steps of contact projection after.
- No dihedral angle test. Accept by the exact objective (unreachable volume of the local window) as now.

Metric gap to fix first (otherwise caps lose)
- The objective counts every unreachable free voxel in the band, including pockets a cap seals shut. A sealed
  void is invisible and needs no milling. Count only unreachable voxels connected to open air through free
  voxels (flood from sd > 0 through free voxels with clearance >= ~0.1 cm). Then sealing a pocket scores as
  a full win, which is what we want for caps.

Other
- Gap threshold 0.2 cm is below the voxel size (0.4). Use cavDx 0.2 on local windows only, or keep 0.4 and
  count sealing as the real criterion.
- Order: cap first (cheapest, biggest gain), then plug what remains, then relocate.
